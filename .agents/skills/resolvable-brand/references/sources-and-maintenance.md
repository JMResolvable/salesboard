## Conflict resolution

Use the user's explicit current instruction first. For logo geometry/colour use the supplied vector master. For current product naming and commercial detail prefer the relevant current page over old search indexing or campaign language. Record an unresolved conflict instead of silently reconciling it. Do not replace exact SVG colours with a raster or CMYK-rendered sample.

## Refresh procedure

Recheck pricing, promotions, product availability and proof whenever publishing relevant claims. Refresh the full skill after a brand update, new asset release or product restructure. Record the date, source and affected reference; retain the prior source/hash when replacing a master. New font evidence should replace the fallback only after family, weights and usage rights are established. Update the version, rerun validation, review the actual visual output and rebuild the portable archive.
