# Brand quality checks

Run the checks relevant to the deliverable. These are a review aid, not an instruction to create extra approval steps.

## Brand and design

- Correct brand/product name, supplied logo variant, aspect ratio, registration mark and readable clear space?
- Exact product colours preserved; symbol colours distinct and unmodified?
- Proposed font clearly identified internally; no claim that a fallback is official?
- Consistent outline icon treatment; proposed icons not misrepresented as official assets?
- Clear hierarchy, calm spacing and mobile/final-print-size legibility?
- Text, meaningful graphics and focus states meet applicable contrast/accessibility needs?

## Content and product

- Is the customer problem and useful next step clear?
- British English, specific verbs, natural warmth and no empty AI hype?
- AI Agents vs human advisors, Data Insights vs Analytics AI, and AI vs Outsourcing distinct?
- No historical product names accidentally promoted as current?
- Demo actions, channels and integrations supported by the brief?
- Draft/suggestion/completed action states accurate?

## Evidence and commercial detail

- Prices and offer dates refreshed if used publicly? VAT, allowances and separate charges clear?
- No savings/performance claim without a suitable source and scope?
- Trial/guarantee conditions verified where relevant?
- Credentials, testimonials and customer marks correctly evidenced and permissioned?
- Example charts and conversations labelled illustrative?
- Source conflicts or remaining gaps stated only where they materially affect use?

## File delivery

Run `python scripts/validate.py` from the package root. Check referenced assets exist and the requested output opens/renders. For responsive work inspect the actual narrow layout; for print inspect a final-size proof. For QR codes verify the destination and scan result. No automated validator can establish that the font or a commercial claim is officially approved.
