# Products and capabilities

Verified from the current public site on 11 November 2026. Descriptions below are concise summaries; availability, connectors and commercial scope must be checked for a specific deployment.

## Architecture

**Resolvable AI** is the software/platform offer. **Resolvable Outsourcing** is the managed people/service offer. They can operate independently or together. Knowledge is a shared operational foundation, not proof that every customer has every module or integration.

The older `/products`, `/products/profile` and indexed legacy material use Robo, Team and Profile. Treat these as historical names for reference; do not use them as today's flagship structure. In particular, do not carry legacy tipping/wallet features into a current Quality description without fresh confirmation.

## Resolvable AI

[AI overview](https://resolvable.com/products/ai): connected customer-service capabilities for handling enquiries, supporting advisors and learning from conversations. Its navigation groups Knowledge & Agent Assist, Quality, Helpdesk, AI Agents, Data Insights, Live Translation, AI Receptionist, Dialler and Analytics AI. Surveys sit alongside quality/customer-feedback workflows.

| Capability | What it does | Keep this boundary | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Knowledge & Agent Assist | Consolidates policies, product information and processes; finds relevant guidance and drafts replies during an enquiry | The advisor reviews guidance and decides what to send; content needs ownership and updates | [Knowledge & Agent Assist](https://resolvable.com/products/ai/knowledge-agent-assist) |
| Quality | Assesses conversations against operation-specific criteria and approved knowledge, with reasoning/evidence for review and coaching | Review scope and exceptions matter; it does not remove human responsibility | [Quality](https://resolvable.com/products/ai/quality-insights) |
| Surveys | Captures the customer's perspective alongside conversation quality | Survey feedback and QA are different measures; do not treat a sample response as a business result | [Quality and surveys](https://resolvable.com/products/ai) |
| Helpdesk | Combines connected email, WhatsApp, social messaging and live chat with tickets, history and ownership | Specific channel connections require actual setup; do not promise universal connectivity | [Helpdesk](https://resolvable.com/products/ai/helpdesk) |
| AI Agents | Uses approved knowledge, connected information and permitted actions for customer enquiries; hands context to a person when needed | Actions are authorised and configured; refunds and exceptions are not unlimited powers | [AI Agents](https://resolvable.com/products/ai/agents) |
| Data Insights | Finds repeated themes, emerging friction and reasons for contact; informs operations, product and knowledge teams | Findings depend on available conversation data; a theme is a prompt to investigate, not proof of cause | [Data Insights](https://resolvable.com/products/ai/data-insights) |
| Live Translation | Supports written messages and two-way spoken exchanges between customer and advisor languages | Confirm languages, channels, quality expectations and enabled scope for the actual deployment | [Live Translation](https://resolvable.com/products/ai/live-translation) |
| AI Receptionist | Answers routine calls from approved knowledge, gathers enquiry details and routes to a person, message or callback | Transfers, hours and permitted answers are configured around the operation | [AI Receptionist](https://resolvable.com/products/ai/voice-bots) |
| Dialler | Organises outbound sales campaigns, prepared lead queues, call outcomes and callbacks | Access is distinct from call charges; do not promise a particular dialling mode without evidence | [Dialler](https://resolvable.com/products/ai/dialler) |
| Analytics AI | Turns plain-language reporting questions into answers and visuals from available operational data | Check measure, source and period; examples are illustrative, not customer performance | [Analytics AI](https://resolvable.com/products/ai/analytics-ai) |

**Data Insights versus Analytics AI:** the former explores themes and friction in customer contact; the latter queries service reporting. Keep that distinction in diagrams and copy.

## Resolvable Outsourcing

[Outsourcing overview](https://resolvable.com/products/outsourcing): managed customer-service agents trained around a client's brand, systems and customer work. Recruitment, preparation and everyday leadership surround the frontline service. Scope and coverage are agreed around the operation.

The current journey runs through understanding the business, recruitment, preparation, launch, support, quality, performance and improvement. Describe staffing, channels, hours, languages, decision boundaries and escalation ownership in a proposal. Flexible/24-hour coverage is available as a service proposition; do not imply every scoped team automatically operates all hours.

Use [brand and tone of voice](https://resolvable.com/products/outsourcing/brand-tone-of-voice) to explain client-specific voice, approved knowledge and authority. Use [pricing](https://resolvable.com/pricing/outsourcing) for inclusion and rate statements. Any optional AI service has separate scope and pricing.

## Example story structure — original recommendation

A customer asks about a missing delivery. Knowledge supplies the approved process. An AI agent checks available tracking and explains the next step. An advisor receives the context when review is needed. Quality identifies a missing communication step; a survey adds customer feedback. Data Insights highlights repeated delivery concerns. Analytics AI helps the service leader examine demand. Outsourcing can provide the people/coverage if required.

Only include the modules, actions and integrations actually supported by the brief. A coherent story is more useful than inserting every capability into every asset.
