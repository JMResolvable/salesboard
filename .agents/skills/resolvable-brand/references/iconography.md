# Iconography and diagrams

## Established sources

The supplied seven-segment symbol is the brand mark. It is not a generic feature icon. The stand uses small outline icons alongside benefits, circular ticks beside feature lists, and black outline pictograms in rounded workflow capsules. Its workflow pictograms suggest knowledge/books, resolution, checking/inspection and improvement/reporting. No official feature-icon library or named third-party icon family was supplied.

## Companion icon system — proposed

`assets/icons/` includes twelve original SVGs authored for this working skill: knowledge, ai-agent, agent-assist, helpdesk, quality, survey, data-insights, translation, receptionist, dialler, analytics and outsourcing. These are **proposed companion icons**, not extracted or approved Resolvable master icons. Use them when a consistent new icon system is needed; preserve existing official icons if subsequently provided.

- 24 × 24 viewBox, 1.75 stroke, round caps and joins, no solid fill by default.
- `currentColor` inherits a meaningful foreground colour. Dark ink on a pale capsule or white on dark is the default.
- Standard sizes: 20/24 px for interfaces, 32/48 px for explanatory diagrams. Test at actual size.
- Keep a single visual weight across a composition. Avoid mixing emoji, filled silhouettes, detailed clip art and outline icons.
- Use teal or pink as a supporting container/accent when the product association helps. Do not recolour the brand symbol to make a feature icon.

## Diagrams

Use the stand's Know → Resolve → Review → Improve loop for an end-to-end story. Label the nodes; use fine arrows and make the return path explicit. Use a simple linear sequence if space cannot support the loop. Put AI and people together in Resolve when both are relevant. Do not draw an arrow from AI straight to a consequential action without showing the applicable approval or handover boundary.

Icons supplement labels. Decorative icons should be hidden from assistive technology; icon-only controls require an accessible name. Preserve at least 3:1 contrast for meaningful graphical objects, and do not communicate status with colour alone.
