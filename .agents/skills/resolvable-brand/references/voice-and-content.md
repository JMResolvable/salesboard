# Voice and content playbook

This is an editorial synthesis of the current site and supplied stand, with original examples. It is not a verbatim copy of a separate tone-of-voice manual.

## Voice

Warm, capable and grounded in the job. Address the reader as “you” and refer to Resolvable as “we” where appropriate. Prefer familiar operational words and specific next steps. Sound like a colleague who understands customer service and can explain what will happen.

| Quality | Use | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Clear | Explain what the tool or team does | Abstract promises about transformation |
| Human | Recognise the customer's problem | Treating people as ticket volume alone |
| Confident | State supported capability plainly | Guarantees without evidence |
| Practical | Give an example and next step | Feature lists without a use case |
| Friendly | Natural contractions and a warm invitation | Forced jokes, slang or excessive exclamation marks |

Use British English, sentence case, active voice and short paragraphs. Use advisor consistently in original copy; retain exact UI/product labels when citing an existing screen. Use “AI agent” for software and “advisor” or “customer service agent” for a person whenever confusion is possible.

The [brand-aligned outsourcing page](https://resolvable.com/products/outsourcing/brand-tone-of-voice) distinguishes a client's voice, approved product knowledge and decision authority. Apply that separation: Resolvable's corporate marketing voice is not automatically the voice of every client's support conversation.

## Content architecture — recommended

Start with a real friction point, show the mechanism, describe the useful outcome, provide evidence and offer one relevant action. In a demo, show the customer request, relevant check, response/action and next owner. Label fictional conversations, figures and charts as illustrative.

## Original copy examples

**AI product intro:** Bring the right knowledge into each conversation. Help customers with routine requests and give your advisors the context to handle what comes next.

**Outsourcing intro:** Build the customer service coverage your business needs, with trained people and day-to-day management around them.

**Quality feature:** Find the step that needs attention. Review the conversation against your standards, then give your team something specific to work on.

**Agent Assist feature:** A useful answer, while the customer is still there. Put relevant guidance beside the conversation so your advisor can review and respond.

**CTA pair:** “Book a demo” with “Explore the platform”; for Outsourcing use “Talk to us” with “See what's included”. Choose one primary action per section.

**Form error:** Enter a work email address so we can reply.

**Success:** Thanks — your enquiry has been sent. [Add an actual response timeframe only if established.]

**Empty state:** No conversations to review yet. Connect a source or choose another date range.

These examples are newly written working copy, not approved testimonials or quotations.

## Channel guidance

- Website: concise value statement, concrete scenario, fit/scope, evidence and action. Keep search intent natural.
- Sales proposal: describe the buyer's operation first; then scope, responsibilities, onboarding, measures and commercial assumptions.
- Email: one reader problem, one useful point and one action. Do not manufacture urgency.
- Social: lead with an observation from customer service, show a small practical example, then invite discussion or exploration.
- Help content: prerequisites, numbered steps, expected result and recovery path. Distinguish a suggestion from a completed action.
- Support conversation: acknowledge the need, explain the check and state the next step. Keep brand warmth appropriate to the customer's emotional state.

## Content themes

Recommended pillars informed by the [blog](https://resolvable.com/blog): practical AI with human oversight; maintaining useful knowledge; honest outsourcing cost comparisons; managing peak demand; quality and coaching; brand consistency; operational improvement from contact data. Build useful guidance around these themes rather than repeating a sales message.

## Do / revise

“Revolutionise CX with our game-changing autonomous ecosystem.” → “Help your team find the right answer and keep the customer moving.”

“Our AI resolves everything instantly.” → “Use AI for approved routine journeys, with a clear handover when a person is needed.”

“Cheap offshore labour.” → “Managed customer service people, trained around your business.”

“The AI has refunded you.” → State that only after the action actually succeeded and was within the configured authority.
