# Brand foundations

## Evidence and interpretation

The current website presents a two-part offer: customer-service AI software and managed customer-service people. The supplied stand presents the same AI/Outsourcing structure. The following positioning and audience framework is a synthesis for consistent production, not a separately supplied strategy document.

## Positioning

Resolvable helps organisations run customer service through connected AI capabilities and managed human support. Position it as practical operational help: better answers, clearer ownership, useful customer insight and people ready to handle the work that needs judgement.

Avoid reducing the brand to a low-cost call centre, a generic chatbot or an autonomous system with unlimited authority. Show a concrete customer need and the information, action or person that moves it forward.

## Message hierarchy

1. Customer outcome: what gets easier or gets resolved?
2. Mechanism: approved knowledge, AI capability, workflow or trained people.
3. Operational fit: the channels, systems, coverage and boundaries involved.
4. Evidence: an actual demonstration, approved result or transparent calculation.
5. One next step: explore the product, book a demonstration or discuss the operation.

Verified short expressions from the [homepage](https://resolvable.com/), retrieved 11 November 2026:

- “Lead with AI. Back it with people.”
- “Customer service, resolved.”
- “AI-led customer service, backed by people.”

Use these sparingly as brand anchors; write original supporting copy for the task.

## Audience framework — recommended

| Reader | Their likely question | Emphasise |
|---|---|---|
| Customer service / CX leader | How will this improve the experience? | Accuracy, ownership, quality, customer feedback |
| Operations leader | How will the work run? | Capacity, coverage, handovers, knowledge and reporting |
| Founder / SME owner | Can I get reliable help without more management? | Manageable starting scope, trained people, practical setup |
| Finance / procurement | What is included and how is it charged? | Separate product prices, scope, allowances, assumptions |
| IT / security | What connects, and who controls actions? | Actual integrations, approved knowledge, permissions, evidence |
| Advisor / team leader | Will this help me do my job? | Relevant answers, clearer context, coaching, less repetitive work |

The [industry index](https://resolvable.com/industries) covers retail/e-commerce, logistics, fashion, healthcare, travel, charity and legal, plus SME and enterprise segments. Use scenarios specific to the audience; do not imply professional advice or unrestricted automation in sensitive workflows.

## Stand framework

The supplied artwork shows a continuous loop:

**Know → Resolve → Review → Improve**

Know: maintained, approved knowledge. Resolve: AI agents and people helping. Review: quality and customer surveys. Improve: insight and coaching feeding back into knowledge and operations. Treat this as a useful narrative framework observed in the stand, not four separately purchasable products.
