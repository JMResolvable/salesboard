# Production recipes — proposed working defaults

These recipes translate the observed identity into new formats. They are recommendations, not templates supplied by the company. Read typography.md before using the fallback font.

## Website / landing page

- Header: appropriate supplied parent/product logo, a small navigation and one clear primary action.
- Hero: dark canvas or white reading surface; short customer outcome, one explanatory paragraph, a clear action and one relevant operational visual.
- Main content: a concrete enquiry journey, 3–5 benefits tied to mechanisms, fit/coverage/integration scope, proof if available and a final next step.
- For a combined offer, give AI teal and Outsourcing pink distinct roles; do not imply a single inclusive commercial package.
- Use semantic headings, keyboard-visible focus, accessible contrast and mobile layouts. Example data must be labelled in the visual itself.
- Do not add fabricated customer logos, numerical claims or mock compliance badges to fill empty sections.

## Presentation / proposal

Suggested sequence: buyer's problem → relevant offer → customer journey → operating model → proof → onboarding/scope → commercial model → next decision. Use one strong idea per slide. Alternate spacious white information slides with dark section/hero slides. Keep a small logo in a consistent position; reserve large lockups for opening/closing slides. Use the supplied mark, not a text substitute.

In proposals, distinguish the buyer's requirements, proposed solution and confirmed commitments. Show who owns knowledge, approvals, escalations and reporting.

## Exhibition / large-format print

Follow the supplied stand's hierarchy: brand, bold offer, a few outcomes, product explanation and action. Use white cards to hold detailed AI/Outsourcing comparisons. Make the headline legible at distance; subordinate fine print must remain readable at final size.

The source PDF is one wide artboard, approximately 600.094 × 158.386 pt. Its filename includes `25%`, but no final physical dimensions, bleed, panel mapping, material or colour profile is established by that. Confirm the printer specification before output. Keep QR codes in a quiet zone, test the encoded destination and scan a final-size proof. Never rebuild a QR code from an exhibition preview.

## Email and social

Use a single product association and a single main action. Lead with a real situation, then a helpful point. Do not force the full product lockup into an unreadable avatar. Add alt text to informative images and keep important copy in accessible text. Short campaign copy can be friendly; procurement or sensitive-service copy should be measured and precise.

## UI and product demo

Make conversation roles, status, checks and next ownership visible. Distinguish draft from sent, suggestion from action and queued from completed. Use a compact status label plus icon/text; avoid colour-only states. Keep fictional personal data plainly illustrative. Provide reduced-motion behaviour and a readable static version of animated sequences.

## Imagery and illustration

Use authorised photography of real people/team activity where available. Use abstract operational diagrams or illustrative interfaces when no genuine photography is supplied. Do not imply a stock/generated person is an actual employee or customer. Keep illustration calm, minimal and grounded in a task; avoid decorative robots and sci-fi clichés.

## Output handoff

Provide the editable source and requested export. Briefly list any substituted font, proposed icon set, unverified claim or production specification that materially affects use. Do not present unresolved choices as official brand standards.
