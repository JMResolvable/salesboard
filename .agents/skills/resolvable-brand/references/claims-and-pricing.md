The page does not establish how long an individual customer's promotional rate lasts.

## Artwork claims are evidence of a campaign, not universal promises

The supplied stand displays a £0 trial with a product-dependent-length/terms footnote, 50%+ cost reduction language, CSAT/NPS improvement, 100% conversation QA language, customer logos, the Resolvable Guarantee badge, ISO marks and GDPR language. These were observed in the artwork. Their presence does not establish eligibility, current terms, certification scope or achieved results for a new customer.

| Claim class | Production rule |
|---|---|
| Cost savings | Use a transparent comparison with the buyer's actual assumptions; distinguish estimated from achieved savings |
| CSAT / NPS / resolution performance | Require approved underlying measurement, dates, population and scope |
| Trial / guarantee | Verify product, length, eligibility, cancellation and material conditions; do not invent guarantee coverage |
| “100%” QA | Clarify eligible conversations and agreed channels/scope; do not imply perfect assessment accuracy |
| Accuracy / availability | Avoid unconditional promises; explain approved knowledge, configuration and human escalation |
| Certifications / GDPR / Cyber Essentials | The site displays ISO 9001, 27001, 22301, Cyber Essentials and GDPR marks; verify entity, status and scope before substantive compliance assertions |
| Customer logos | Obtain/use appropriate permission and correct artwork; a displayed logo does not prove a specific result |
| Integration | A named tool or logo does not establish a native connector, every feature or every plan; confirm actual setup |

The [website terms](https://resolvable.com/terms) distinguish public illustrative tools from production performance or a commercial quote. Keep sample charts and conversations labelled accordingly.

Prefer useful scope statements over generic disclaimers. Put material conditions beside the claim they qualify. Keep internal evidence notes out of final marketing layouts unless the reader needs them to understand the offer.
